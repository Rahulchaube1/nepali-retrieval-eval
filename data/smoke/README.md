# Software fixtures only

These eight documents and twelve queries are AI-authored artificial strings for testing ingestion, Unicode handling, ranking and scoring. The Devanagari characters are symbols in mechanical fixtures, not translated sentences. All queries use the `control` variant. Every row is marked `synthetic_fixture` and `unreviewed`.

The collection has ten answerable queries and two deliberately unanswerable queries. Its predetermined relevance labels are software-test expectations, not human linguistic judgments. It contains no BOUQuET or FLORES+ text. Do not use its scores to compare language-model quality or claim Nepali coverage.
